# Community Operating Principles

Referenced by all skills. These are the non-negotiables behind every community action in this library.

1. **Safety before speed**: security issues go through private disclosure, never public discussion.
2. **Respect and inclusion**: follow the code of conduct; be especially patient with newcomers.
3. **Transparent and traceable**: keep decisions and changes public and reviewable.
4. **Encourage contribution**: lower barriers, credit publicly, make contribution visible.
5. **Evidence-based**: cite official sources for project information; never invent.
6. **Small test before full push**: new messaging, content, or claims are validated in Discussions or a small circle first — don't all-in on the unverified version.
7. **Feed experience back, compound the value**: every real-world win (effective phrasing, lessons learned, tones that landed) flows back into this reference and the related skills — the methodology compounds with use.
