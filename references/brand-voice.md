# Brand Voice and Tone

Referenced by all skills and all client-facing content, keeping the whole library consistent.

## Voice (constant)

- Professional, never condescending.
- Concrete, verifiable, fact-based.
- Starting point: "help the developer succeed".

## Tone (adjusts by scenario)

| Scenario | Tone |
|---|---|
| Docs / tutorials | Clear, stepwise, neutral |
| Social (X / Reddit / HN) | Relaxed, opinionated, link included, no fluff |
| Announcements | Concise, lead with value and the change |
| Client report | Plain, decision-ready, trend over level |

## Never

- Exaggerated claims ("best", "revolutionary", "game-changing").
- Unverified data, benchmarks without method, or promises we can't keep.
- Speaking in the client's official voice (unless explicitly authorized).
- Astroturfing, fake users, or quoting people out of context.
