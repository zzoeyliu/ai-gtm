# Marketing Operating Principles / 市场运作原则

Referenced by all skills. These are the non-negotiables behind every GTM action in this library.
被所有 skill 引用。这是本库每个市场动作背后不可妥协的原则。

1. **Outcomes over output / 重结果而非产出**: we measure whether users adopt and convert, not whether they liked the post. Signups and activation beat impressions.
   我们衡量用户是否转化，而非是否喜欢内容。注册与激活胜过曝光。

2. **Evidence-based / 基于证据**: cite real signals, benchmarks, and proof; never invent metrics, claims, or testimonials.
   引用真实信号、基准与证明；绝不编造数据、主张或证言。

3. **Small test before full push / 先小范围验证再全力推**: validate message, creative, or channel before spending the full budget. Don't all-in on an unverified assumption.
   在花光预算前先验证信息、创意或渠道。别把赌注压在未验证的假设上。

4. **Audience respect / 尊重用户**: no hype, no fake "best", no jargon without translation. Speak the audience's language; earn trust with specifics.
   不炒作、不假称「最佳」、黑话必翻译。说用户听得懂的话，用具体事实换信任。

5. **AI is a lever, not autopilot / AI 是杠杆不是自动驾驶**: AI drafts, analyzes, and scales; a human owns the message, the judgment, and the client relationship.
   AI 负责起草、分析、放大；人掌握信息、判断与客户关系。

6. **Client confidentiality / 客户保密**: protect client data and unreleased plans; never disclose without consent.
   保护客户数据与未公开计划；未经同意绝不披露。

7. **Feed experience back, compound the value / 经验回流，价值复利**: every campaign's wins and misses flow back into these skills — the playbook compounds with use.
   每次活动的得失都回流到这些 skill——打法越用越厚。
