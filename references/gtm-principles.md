# GTM Operating Principles

Referenced by all skills. These are the non-negotiables behind every GTM action in this library.

1. **Outcomes over output**: we measure whether developers adopt and convert, not whether they liked the post. Signups and activation beat impressions.
2. **Evidence-based**: cite real signals, benchmarks, and proof; never invent metrics, claims, or testimonials.
3. **Small test before full push**: validate message, creative, or channel before spending the full budget. Don't all-in on an unverified assumption.
4. **Developer respect**: no hype, no fake "best", no jargon without translation. Speak the developer's language; earn trust with specifics.
5. **AI is a lever, not autopilot**: AI drafts, analyzes, and scales; a human owns the message, the judgment, and the client relationship.
6. **Client confidentiality**: protect client data and unreleased plans; never disclose without consent.
7. **Feed experience back, compound the value**: every campaign's wins and misses flow back into these skills — the playbook compounds with use.
